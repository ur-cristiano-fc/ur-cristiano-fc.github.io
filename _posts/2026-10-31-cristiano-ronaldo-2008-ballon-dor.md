### The Ballon d'Or Ceremony: A Deserved Victory

After such a breathtaking season, it was no surprise that Cristiano Ronaldo was the clear favorite for the `2008 Ballon d'Or winner`. The award, given by France Football magazine, recognizes the best player in the world. On December 2, 2008, in Paris, Ronaldo was officially crowned.

He received a staggering 446 points out of a possible 480 votes. This was a massive lead over second-place Lionel Messi (281 points) and third-place Fernando Torres (179 points). It wasn't just a win; it was a landslide. The decision was unanimous amongst experts and fans alike.

This `Cristiano Ronaldo 2008 Ballon d'Or` marked his first of many. It was a moment that cemented his place at the very top of world football.
